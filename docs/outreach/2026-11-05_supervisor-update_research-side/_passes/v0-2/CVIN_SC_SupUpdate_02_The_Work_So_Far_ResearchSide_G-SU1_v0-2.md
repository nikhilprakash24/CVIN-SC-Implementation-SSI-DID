# CVIN-SC: The Work So Far — the research side

Oct 31, 2026 (freeze) · Nikhil Prakash · v0-2 · **Companion to the code-side document of the same title. That document covers the applied system; this one covers the research corpus, the specification and the evaluation of record that the thesis is built from.**

## What this is

CVIN-SC asks what an identity layer for connected and autonomous vehicles must minimally provide, whether the blockchain identity standards now in print provide it, and whether the answer survives the move from vehicles to software agents. The research side answers those questions by derivation, by a standards inventory and by a pre-registered evaluation of eight implementations of one interface; the code side (the companion document) answers a different and complementary set by building and measuring a full vehicular system. Every research-side number in the thesis is tied to a committed trace in the hub repository, `nikhilprakash24/cvin-sc` (private; access on request), and every claim carries one of three status words: *verdict* (gate-passed, resolves to a committed trace), *expectation* (predicted, or from an unrun or superseded instrument) or *analysis* (derived without measurement).

This document lists what the research side does that I have not found done elsewhere, what it found, where it was frozen, where each piece lives, and where the thesis stands.

## What this side does that I have not found elsewhere

Each item is stated for what it is: derived, measured or argued, with its status, and positioned against the literature in Chapter 2. Priority claims invite counter-examples, so none is phrased as "first".

1. **A derived, not assembled, minimal identity interface.** `IMinimalSSI`, twelve functions, derived from a 29-operation universe assembled from the W3C DID Core and VC Data Model normative operations and Allen's ten principles, under a published clustering rule (CR-1…CR-3), by a two-part necessity argument. The result is stated as a *sufficient and irredundant cover*, explicitly not the unique minimum; alternatives under a coarser rule fall to nine or ten functions without touching the credential operations. The derivation is mechanically checkable by re-running two elimination tests on the committed operation map. *(analysis)*
2. **The necessity partition and "no thirteenth function".** Three functions are principle-necessary (`createDID`, `resolveDID`, `createPresentation`), five operation-necessary, four conventionally necessary — and all the conventionality sits in key management. Seven principles are discharged on the function layer and three off it, each at a named site. Presentation *verification* folds into `verifyCredential`; there is no thirteenth function. *(analysis; CEV-16…18)*
3. **A closure relation for a complete identity system.** "Complete" is defined as *closed under the stated lifecycle transitions over the exhibited object set*, with a falsification condition, a role table (issuer, holder, verifier, VDR, controller, delegate) and a lifecycle closure table. The analysis found one closure failure — delegate revocation before expiry — and resolved it by Specification Amendment F11, corroborated in registry code. It is the only place the analysis changed the specification. *(analysis, falsifiable)*
4. **`createPresentation` as the sole discharge of Consent and Minimization — three claims that fail independently.** Necessity (analytic, §2.3); absence from the ERC register (a dated census, not a literature search: provisional count over 30 ERCs, none native, 17 partial, 13 absent); and per-option realisation (on every EVM option the function is a replay-protected anchor, scored *absent* by the principle-preservation rule). *(analysis · expectation until the census is dated · verdict for ERC-1056)*
5. **A standards inventory on one footing.** Seventeen standalone options in six families (ERC registries, tokens, accounts, agent registries, the Indy/Aries and Identus sub-layers of Family E, and the Family F composite O18), each profiled against the twelve functions at a freeze date, with a standards-symmetry table (DID operations ↔ Set A; VC lifecycle ↔ Set B), a Family E sub-layer decomposition and a Family G census of ERC-1056 industry lineages. *(framework; inventory partly issued)*
6. **A pre-registered evaluation protocol, with its disclosure.** Fixed by commit `0a10831` on 15 October 2026 before any option other than ERC-1056 had been ported: measurands, N = 5 per gas arm and N = 30 per timing, seven void rules, a Full/Partial/Absent rubric with four tests, self-audit controls. The eight-option runs made after it but not under it are admitted by ruling D-R7 with the deviation stated in the thesis's own voice (a dated note in §2.5); the eleven ERC-1056 runs that pre-date the protocol, two within ten minutes of the commit, are identified and still to be listed by a dated amendment; gas was then re-measured under the protocol (D-R8: 128 of 128 cells at N = 5, every repetition identical; deployment gas at N = 5 on all fourteen rows). *(method; verdict for the re-measurement)*
7. **One reference result that reproduces to the digit.** ERC-1056 passes 12/12 under a pinned compiler (solc 0.8.24), and eleven of its twelve transaction gas values are identical across three runs over four and a half months and two compiler states; the twelfth varies for one identified reason (an unseeded challenge, −12 gas per zero byte). The 17,100-gas difference between the first and later identity writes is exactly one fresh-slot SSTORE (22,100 − 5,000), visible inside one trace on every option. *(verdict)*
8. **Eight native ports of one interface, admitted by one unmodified suite.** ERC-1056, ERC-721, ERC-725, ERC-725+735, ERC-1155, ERC-4337, ERC-8004 and Indy-on-Besu each pass the same twelve tests; scenario code written above the interface runs unchanged on all eight. Coverage and gas order the standards differently (native functions of twelve: 9, 9, 8, 6, 6, 5, 3, 2; creation gas from ≈0.16 M on light registries to ≈1.63 M on account-per-identity designs). Eighteen interface-level findings change the specification rather than the numbers: entitlement, content binding, supersession, controller transfer and early delegate revocation lie outside the twelve functions. *(pre-protocol, admitted; findings as existence and impossibility results)*
9. **Two independent analyses that draw the same boundary.** An on-chain/off-chain placement procedure (eight statement classes, seven placement forms, four tests in order — act, personal, equivocation, Tier-2 read) puts Set A state on-chain and Set B content off-chain and anchored; the minimality derivation drew the same Set A / Set B line for different reasons. Hash-only anchoring is both the default and the cheapest form; throughput is shown by arithmetic not to be the binding constraint, which sends the argument to latency and privacy. *(analysis)*
10. **The agentic generalisation as an interface claim.** A mapping φ from vehicular to agent principals that preserves the interface and not the trust model: five functions transfer exactly, seven modified, none absent; nine non-correspondences and two design rules named; an ERC-4337/ERC-7579 smart-account wrapper specified module by module with a pre-declared validation ladder. Secure identity-to-identity messaging for agents was the subject of the three memos that re-opened this programme (project listing dates them 25 January 2026; any precedence claim is to be made from dated artefacts, not from this sentence). *(analysis; wrapper at specification only)*

Beneath these, a corrections ledger: the research side found and corrected figures that earlier material you have seen carried wrongly — most importantly the BSM broadcast rate (10 Hz nominal, not 1,000–2,000 per second; the larger figure is receive-side load) and the Groth16 on-chain verification cost (≈181,000 + 6,150 per public input after EIP-1108, not ≈350,000). These are reported, not silently replaced.

## The freeze point

The research side froze with the code side on 31 October 2026, for the same reason: the field is moving (VC Data Model 2.0 at Recommendation since May 2025; DID 1.1 at Candidate Recommendation since March 2026; ERC-4337 and ERC-8004 still settling; EDPB guidance of July 2026 breaking the "GDPR by hash-anchoring" sentence that earlier drafts carried) and the work has moved with it. The frozen state is the hub repository at `d9f9fa3` (31 October, the unified-thesis ruling D-R9); the project store stood at 168 filed documents when this was written (4–5 November), with only reporting instruments added since the freeze. What arrives after the freeze — a new standard, a new ruling, a new draft — goes to the programme around the thesis unless it corrects a number of record.
