Security — Two-Lens Adversarial Register

attack_results.json · security_matrix.json · 54 executed attacks · solc 0.8.24 · OZ 5.0.2 · Hardhat-local

Two complementary security lenses over the nine standards. Lens A is an executed attack suite — real malicious transactions sent and their reverts captured. Lens B is a comparative threat-posture matrix that surfaces the security/performance trade-offs behind H5 (no single standard dominates).

What it measures
Lens A (attack_results.json): 9 standards × 6 attack classes (unauthorized issuance / attribute-write / delegate-or-claim / revocation, identity hijack, signature replay) = 54 cells. Lens B (security_matrix.json): 9 standards × 6 threat categories (impersonation, replay, identity theft, sybil, recovery, privacy) across on-chain + off-chain layers.
Method
Every Lens-A cell is a real on-chain outcome: the malicious tx was actually sent and its revert reason recorded (method=executed) or a structural absence argued from verified source (method=reasoned). Lens B grades each cell DEFENDED / PARTIAL / VULNERABLE / N/A and pairs it with the demonstrated mechanism and evidence string.
Key numbers
Of the 54 attack cells, 11 are N/A (no gated step to bypass) and all 43 applicable scenarios are DEFENDED — 43/43, zero vulnerable. The comparative matrix is deliberately not all-green: 21 DEFENDED, 15 PARTIAL, 14 VULNERABLE, 4 N/A on-chain, showing where each standard sits on the frontier.
Source file
4_comparison-framework/security-analysis/results/attack_results.json, security_matrix.json (+ onchain_security.json)
Provenance / caveat
The audit found MOBI attestEvent stored an attestation signature it never verified (a forgery/replay gap); the researcher added on-chain ecrecover over a domain-separated digest (contract + chainId + vehicle + eventId, EIP-191, OZ ECDSA low-s), with a before/after regression test. Caveat: the adversary model is operational, not formalized (RESEARCH_AUDIT §4.7); the matrix records PARTIAL/VULNERABLE cells honestly rather than claiming universal defense.
Found & fixed

All 43 applicable attacks (of 54) are defended, with every revert captured from a real transaction. The honesty asset: the suite caught our own bug — attestEvent now verifies its signature on-chain, moving the MOBI Replay cell from PARTIAL to DEFENDED at a cost of 121,110 → 192,718 gas. (Security §5.6.)
