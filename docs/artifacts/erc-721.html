ERC-721 — NFT Vehicle Identity

EIP-721 · non-fungible token · on-chain metadata struct

The token-as-identity model: each vehicle is a unique NFT whose VIN, metadata and transfer history live in contract state. Evaluated as the heavy-state end of the spectrum against the ERC-1056 baseline.

What it is
The canonical non-fungible token standard: every vehicle is a distinct token with a single owner. In this implementation minting also writes a VIN mapping, a full on-chain metadata struct and an initial transfer record — identity data is stored, not just referenced.
Reference
eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-721
Role in thesis
The NFT-based vehicle-identity substrate; one of the nine compared standards and a primary illustration of the on-chain-metadata cost that hypothesis H1 predicts.
Where used
1_blockchain-identity/contracts/ERC721/CVINVehicleNFT.sol
Provenance
Standard selection and the comparative framing are the researcher's (SOURCES.md §1). The heavy-metadata mintVehicle design — storing the VIN, metadata and transfer history on-chain — was the researcher-directed choice to make the storage cost explicit and measurable; implemented and measured by the toolchain under that direction (PROVENANCE.md).
H1 · cost

Creating an identity costs 542,429 gas on ERC-721 — about 10.4× the ERC-1056 baseline (52,612). The premium is the on-chain metadata struct and transfer record; this is the heavy-state case that hypothesis H1 is measured against. (Results §5.2.)
