MOBI VID I — Vehicle Birth Certificate

MOBI Vehicle Identity · W3C VC · keccak256 content-hash anchor · salted-SHA256 + AES-256-GCM VIN

The birth-certificate credential model: a manufacturer's record of a vehicle's origin, realized as a W3C Verifiable Credential whose integrity is anchored on-chain by a keccak256 content hash, with VIN privacy provided by a salted SHA-256 commitment and an AES-256-GCM ciphertext — never a plaintext VIN.

What it is
The MOBI Vehicle Identity (VID) birth-certificate model: an immutable, non-expiring credential asserting a vehicle's origin claims (VIN, make, model, year, manufacturing date, manufacturer DID). Issued by an authorized manufacturer and bound to a did:ethr vehicle identity.
Reference
dlt.mobi — MOBI Vehicle Identity (VID)
Role in thesis
The privacy-preserving birth-certificate profile of the MOBI-VID application profile measured alongside the nine identity standards. Realized as a W3C VC with content-hash anchoring, not IPFS: the contract's birthCertHash stores keccak256 of the canonicalized signed VC; the credential itself stays off-chain with the holder (data minimization).
Where used
2_w3c-ssi-layer/mobi-vid/birth_certificate.py
Provenance
Implemented per the MOBI VID model by the researcher. The privacy design is the researcher's: vinHash = sha256(vin || ":" || salt) defeats VIN-space enumeration, the AES-256-GCM ciphertext is AEAD-bound to the on-chain vinHash (key derived via HKDF-SHA256, never on-chain), and the DID is did:ethr:<chainId>:<address> — no VIN in the identifier. See PROVENANCE.md.
Privacy · §5.6

MOBI VID is the only compared family that avoids plaintext VINs on-chain: it stores a salted SHA-256 hash plus an AES-256-GCM ciphertext and emits only the hash, while ERC-721/ERC-735/ERC-1155/LSP8 write the raw VIN to public state or events. Measured across the 5-backend sweep (§5.3.1) and confirmed on-chain by the two-lens security analysis. (Results §5.6.)
