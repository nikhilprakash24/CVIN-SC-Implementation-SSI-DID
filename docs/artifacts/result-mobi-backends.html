MOBI VID Cross-Backend Sweep (H4)

mobi_vid_backends.json · 5 backends × 3 ops · fidelity/5 · solc 0.8.24 · OZ 5.0.2 · Hardhat-local

Realizes the MOBI VID identity + lifecycle model on five different blockchain backends using each backend's native primitives, measuring both the gas per operation and a fidelity score. Direct evidence for H4: MOBI VID maps most economically onto event-log standards and most faithfully onto claim-based standards, with the hybrid on the fidelity-per-gas frontier.

What it measures
Three operations — birth attestation (VID I), lifecycle event (VID II), and third-party attestation — on ERC-1056, ERC-735, ERC-1155, CVIN-Combined, and MOBI-VID-V2, each scored for fidelity across five concepts (birth anchoring, lifecycle events, multi-party attestation, verifiable claims, revocation).
Method
One representative tx per (backend, operation) on a fresh Hardhat network, using each backend's existing contract. Cells marked approximate flag a forced mapping where the backend genuinely lacks the concept (its gas is the closest honest analogue, not a faithful realization). Fidelity = count of natively-supported concepts out of 5.
Key numbers
Birth / lifecycle gas is cheapest on the event-log backends — CVIN-Combined 51,728 / 34,652, ERC-1056 51,962 / 34,946 — vs MOBI-VID-V2's purpose-built structs (298,923 / 306,980). Fidelity is 3/5 for ERC-1056 and ERC-1155 (no native multi-party attestation) and 5/5 for ERC-735, CVIN-Combined, and MOBI-VID-V2. MOBI's attestEvent (192,694) is the cheapest native attestation, under ERC-735 (290,212) and CVIN-Combined (334,883).

Provenance / caveat
Produced by 1_blockchain-identity/scripts/mobi_vid_backend_sweep.js under the researcher's direction. Caveat: the two 3/5 backends' third-party-attestation cells are honest approximations — ERC-1056's signer must be the identity owner (not an independent party) and ERC-1155 mints a parallel cert with no on-chain linkage; neither is a faithful multi-party attestation.
Supports H4

Only backends with an on-chain, signature-verified claim or attestation reach 5/5 fidelity — and the CVIN-Combined hybrid reaches it at the lowest total cost, paying the cheap event-log price (51,728 / 34,652) on the common path and the 334,883-gas claim only when a safety-critical on-chain attestation is actually needed.
