### FIG-R15 — V2X safety-message bottleneck (new)
- **File / source:** `FIG-R15_v2x_bottleneck_v0-2.svg` · R7 v0-2 §4.2 (PF-43) and §4.3 (PF-44). The numbers are parsed from the `[PF-43]` and `[PF-44]` lines of `r7_calc_v0-2.py`.
- **How to read:**
  - Each line is the verification time per 100 ms BSM interval (ρ_v × 100) against the neighbour count P, for five verifier classes: Xeon SSI and PKI warm (M, #27); V2X ASIC/HSM, Cortex-A53 software and MCU HSM (E). The register #26 linear model is also drawn (P\*(0.5) = 305, the lead figure).
  - Crossing the dashed 50 ms line (f_id = 0.5) gives P\*. Crossing the red 100 ms Tier-1 line means the queue is unstable. The MCU HSM saturates at P = 20 and the A53 class at P = 100.
  - Purple verticals show how many vehicles an 802.11p channel carries at CBR 0.6: 37 (repo JSON PKI), 63 (repo JSON ERC-1056) and 182 (1609.2 digest).
  - Conclusion: verification hardware and airtime bind before the ledger. The ledger enters only through refreshes: k_f ≥ 27.9 under the refresh-load criterion, and k_f ≥ 91 under the whole-path criterion (the #32 knee).
- **Caveats:**
  - The Xeon rows are a server host, not an OBU (NC-05).
  - The hardware classes are E (LIM-R7-03).
  - The PF-44 sizes are E (LIM-R7-02/07).
  - The #26 line is extrapolated beyond the tested P ≤ 80 (DRP-03).
- **Changed from v0-1:** new.

### FIG-R16 — Revocation latency tail (new)
- **File / source:** `FIG-R16_revocation_tail_v0-2.svg` · R7 v0-2 §4.8 (PF-48). All nine (μ_off, A_L) cells are parsed from the `[PF-48]` lines of `r7_calc_v0-2.py`.
- **How to read:**
  - The bars show E[W], p99 and p99.9 on a log scale for μ_off = 10, 60 and 600 s and A_L = 0.99, 0.95 and 0.90, with W_0 = 8.5 s (R4 PF-28) dashed.
  - The mean barely moves while the tail explodes: at 99 % availability with 10-minute outages, E[W] is 14.5 s but p99.9 is 1390.1 s (about 23 min).
  - Guarantees must therefore be stated as tail quantiles. The poll-period additions and the MC agreement (1.34 %) are printed from the script.
- **Caveats:**
  - The whole model is Ds (stationary renewal, exponential down-times), and no backhaul was measured.
  - p99 = W_0 wherever 1 − A_L ≤ 0.01.
  - The A_L = 0.99, q = 0.99 edge cell is a sampling artefact in the MC and is excluded.
- **Changed from v0-1:** new.
