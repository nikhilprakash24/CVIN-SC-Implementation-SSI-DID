## 6. Gotchas learned
- **Ports.** The MOBI pytest fixture starts its own node on **8547**; give the testbed node another
  port (CI uses 8548).
- **Killing nodes.** Never `pkill -f` with a pattern that also matches your own shell command; kill
  by PID.
- **Scripts that overwrite tracked results:** `attack_scenarios.py`, `sumo_identity_integration.py
  --simulate`, `run_verify_richness.py`, `benchmark_gas.js` (date and attestEvent note only). Restore
  with `git checkout` unless you mean to update them.
- **Selector shifts.** Changing any function signature in a contract shifts dispatch gas for its
  other functions by tens of gas (K-1 moved `createVehicleDID` +22). Re-run the gas gates after any
  ABI change.
- **Library versions.** `cryptography` 41 → 49 alone made the PKI cells 2–4× faster. Record library
  versions with every latency run (the experiment does).
