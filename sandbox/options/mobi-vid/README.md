# MOBI VID I + II — feature demos (plan S7)

**Identity model.** A vehicle is an ERC-1056 identity *address* (its own key) in a shared `MOBIVIDRegistryV2`; an authorised manufacturer anchors a VID I birth certificate to it — a VIN **hash**, an **encrypted** VIN, a certificate hash and the first owner, who becomes the ERC-1056 `owner` — and from then on the owner manages delegates/attributes (event-only, ERC-1056 style), sells with odometer-stamped `transferVehicleOwnership`, and role-authorised issuers (8 `IssuerRole`s) record 11 `EventType`s of VID II lifecycle events that other issuers can co-sign with on-chain-verified `attestEvent`. The Python provider adds the off-chain V2X hot path (`sign_message`/`verify_message`, SHA-256 + secp256k1 over raw digests).

Run one demo: `cd 1_blockchain-identity && npx hardhat run ../sandbox/options/mobi-vid/demos/<family>.js`. `demos/_lib.js` is the shared step printer. 252 steps, 80 mined transactions.

## Asymmetry notes

- **Creation: minted/registered.** The identity address is a key the vehicle (or the adapter, deterministically from the VIN) chooses off-chain; it becomes a vehicle only through the manufacturer's `registerVehicleBirth` (277 k gas in L1). Birth hands ERC-1056 control to `firstOwner`, so the vehicle's own key loses `owner` status at birth. The ERC-1056 `registerVehicle` path (self-registration by publishing a veriKey attribute) is disjoint from VID I and can be repeated.
- **Veracity and automation.** The chain enforces the manufacturer set, the owner on every ERC-1056 write, the 11×8 role matrix on `recordLifecycleEvent`, existence/non-revocation checks, and — unique among the compared options — an **on-chain signature check** on `attestEvent` bound to (registry, chainId, vehicle, eventId). It stores event structs, counters, odometer series, ownership records and attestations, so history is queryable without logs. It does not enforce odometer monotonicity, does not link the OWNER role to `identityOwner`, does not re-check issuer status on read, and `changeOwner`/`DECOMMISSION` bypass the VID II history and the revoked flag respectively. ERC-1056 delegates/attributes remain event-only (no getters, no `validDelegate`), so DID-document resolution is log replay.
- **Cryptography / hashing.** VIN privacy: `vinHash` is whatever bytes32 the caller supplies (provider: SHA-256 with salt; adapter: keccak256 — uniqueness therefore holds only per convention) plus an opaque encrypted VIN. `attestEvent`: `keccak256(abi.encodePacked(...))` under the **EIP-191** envelope, OZ `ECDSA.recover` (low-s enforced), must equal `msg.sender`; a raw-digest signature is rejected. Off-chain messaging (provider): SHA-256 of canonical JSON, secp256k1 ECDSA over the **raw digest** (no EIP-191, no chain id), verified against the public key embedded in the message. Attribute names are bytes32 (`encodeBytes32String` or `keccak256`), event ids `keccak256(vehicle, type, issuer, timestamp, count)`.
- **Implemented but never compared.** All EventTypes except MAINTENANCE/REPAIR and all IssuerRoles except SERVICE_CENTER, the `verified` flag, multi-party attestations and their negative space, `getEventsByType`/`getOdometerHistory`/`getEventTypeCount`/`getVehicleEvents`/`getEvent`, raw getters, `changeOwner` vs `transferVehicleOwnership`, `updateVehicleKey`, `revokeAttribute`/`revokeDelegate`, `birthAttributes`, manufacturer/issuer/authority administration, `getVehicleDID`/`getVehicleInfo`, VIN decryption, and the entire off-chain hot path.

## Coverage check

Union of functions exercised across the eleven demos vs the public/external ABI: **MOBIVIDRegistryV2 54/54 (which includes the full MOBIVIDRegistry and ERC1056Registry surface) — no uncovered function.** Provider-only methods (`sign_message`, `verify_message`, `export_vin_key`, `*_credential`, `resolve_identity`) are mirrored, not executed, because they are Python.

Behaviour worth flagging (reproduced): `getVehicleDID` omits the `0x` prefix of the address (non-conformant did:ethr, copied verbatim by the adapter); the provider's `verify_message` accepts an impostor who embeds its own public key under the vehicle's DID (no chain binding); `revokeIdentity` can be repeated and moves `revokedAt`; `changeOwner` works on a revoked identity and bypasses ownership history; `revokeAttribute` accepts a value different from the one set; the same attester can attest the same event repeatedly; the OWNER role is registry-wide; `transferRegistryAuthority` emits no event; `nonce` is dead state.
