# Research copies (archived 2026-10-04)

These directories are the original CVIN-ID-SCs research material cloned in the first
commit of this repository (`b23bc4c`, 2025-11-10): Truffle-era ERC-721 contracts and
tests, the ERC-725 draft, the ERC-725xy notes, the ERC-1056 comparison notes
(`ERC1056/*.md`) and the ERC-1055 note. They are **not compiled, not tested and not
measured**; Hardhat's `paths.sources` is `./contracts`, which is the canonical tree.

They are kept, unmodified, for provenance (`PROVENANCE.md`, `docs/DEVELOPMENT_HISTORY.md`)
and because the sandbox plan credits them as the origin of several design decisions.
Nothing should import from here. See `docs/PLAN_SANDBOX_AND_SUITES.md` S0.
