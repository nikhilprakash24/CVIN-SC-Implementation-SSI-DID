Questions we get asked

Not in the deck
Why hasn't an incumbent fleet-management vendor built this?

Incumbents sell to the fleet owner on fuel and routing. Maintenance sits with a different buyer, the shop manager, and needs parts, labor and warranty workflows the incumbents have never had to model. Two have tried through acquisition and both products were sunset within three years.

What happens to churn when a fleet is acquired?

Eleven of our fleets have been acquired since 2024. Nine kept Northwind; in six of those the acquirer rolled it out to additional depots. The two losses were to acquirers already standardized on an ERP maintenance module.

How dependent is the product on telematics data?

About 60% of fleets connect a telematics feed; the rest run on odometer entries from the shop. The product works without the feed, but connected fleets show a 30% higher expansion rate, so onboarding pushes hard for the integration.

What is the plan if the round takes longer than expected?

Current runway at the present burn is 14 months. The plan with no new capital reaches cash-flow breakeven in 11 months by freezing the second onboarding team hire, at the cost of roughly a third of forecast growth.
