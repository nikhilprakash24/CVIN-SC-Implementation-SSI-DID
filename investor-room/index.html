Questions we get asked

Not in the deck
Why can't the DMS vendors do this themselves?

The DMS is a ledger: it records that a vehicle was reconditioned, not what should happen next. Reconditioning runs across service, parts, detail and outside vendors, and needs a scheduling and accountability layer the DMS vendors have never built. Two have partnered with us rather than compete.

What happens in a used-car downturn?

Days to front line matter more when margins compress, not less. In the 2023 inventory correction our oldest cohort expanded rooftops by 19%. The product is paid from recon savings that exist regardless of the sale price.

How concentrated is the dealer group market?

The top 150 groups own about a quarter of US franchise rooftops. We have 5 of the top 150 and 38 groups in total; the long tail of 10 to 40 rooftop groups is where most of the pipeline sits.

What breaks if onboarding can't scale?

Growth caps at roughly 120 rooftops a quarter with the current four teams. This round funds four more teams and a remote-install path for groups already on a supported DMS.
